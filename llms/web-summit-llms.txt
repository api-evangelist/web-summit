# Web Summit
Web Summit runs the world’s largest technology events, connecting people and ideas that change the world. It takes place in Lisbon’s MEO Arena in November.


## When is Web Summit?
Web Summit 2026 will take place in the MEO Arena in Lisbon, November 9-12, 2026. 


## Where is Web Summit?
At the MEO Arena, in Lisbon, Portugal. The address as it appears on Google is ‘Rossio dos Olivais, 1990-231 Lisboa, Portugal’.


## Who attends Web Summit?
Web Summit 2025 gathered 71,000 people, including speakers and attendees from 157 countries. For more information about who is coming from your country, visit [Web Summit’s featured attendee page](https://websummit.com/featured-attendees/).

## Is Web Summit worth it?
Web Summit offers an excellent opportunity to network with people, learn about technology trends, and hear from industry leaders.
You can find verified Web Summit attendee reviews at [websummit.com/reviews/](https://websummit.com/reviews/).

## Content tracks at Web Summit
Web Summit organizes its content into 20 distinct content tracks, allowing attendees to focus on the topics most relevant to their interests. Your ticket gives you access to all of them.
Here are the key content tracks featured at the conference in 2025:
### AI Summit
AI Summit explores the most dominant topic in the tech industry: the world-changing potential and risks of artificial intelligence. It covers the line between innovation and instability, addressing how AI is designing intelligence and disrupting society.
#### Topics related to this content track include: Generative AI, Machine Learning, AI Ethics, Automation, AI Regulation.
[websummit.com/tracks/ai-summit](https://websummit.com/tracks/ai-summit)


### SaaS Summit
SaaS Summit focuses on the evolution of Software-as-a-Service, a cornerstone of modern business. It discusses the future of the industry, including AI-first workflows, composable platforms that allow for greater flexibility, and a necessary rethinking of subscription models to stay competitive.
#### Topics related to this content track include: Cloud Computing, Subscription Models, B2B Tech, AI-First Workflows, Composable Platforms.
[websummit.com/tracks/saas-summit](https://websummit.com/tracks/saas-summit)


### Fintech Summit
Fintech Summit dives into the rapidly evolving world of financial technology. Key discussions include the scaling of digital and real-time payments, the critical role of AI in fraud prevention and personalization, and navigating the complex landscape of financial regulation.
#### Topics related to this content track include: Digital Payments, Cryptocurrency, Blockchain, AI in Finance, Regulation.
[websummit.com/tracks/fintech-summit/](https://websummit.com/tracks/fintech-summit/)


### Commerce Summit
Commerce Summit focuses on digital transformation in retail and e-commerce, this track examines how massive e-commerce growth is forcing companies to innovate. It covers modernizing supply chains and creating new ways to engage with customers in a crowded digital marketplace.
#### Topics related to this content track include: E-commerce, Retail Tech, Supply Chain, Customer Engagement, Digital Innovation.
[websummit.com/tracks/commerce-summit](https://websummit.com/tracks/commerce-summit)


### Marketing Summit
Marketing Summit is an essential track for any business and explores the shift towards data-driven, personalized marketing. It highlights how purpose-driven campaigns are becoming crucial to meet consumer demands for greater ethics and transparency from brands.
#### Topics related to this content track include: Digital Marketing, Data Analytics, Personalization, Brand Strategy, and Consumer Ethics.
[websummit.com/tracks/marketing-summit/](https://websummit.com/tracks/marketing-summit/)


### Growth Summit
Growth Summit is both for startups and established companies alike, this track covers the fundamentals of business leadership and resilience. It addresses everything from securing initial funding and building investor confidence to developing go-to-market strategies and driving sustainable growth.
#### Topics related to this content track include: Startups, Venture Capital, Scaling, Leadership, Go-to-Market Strategy.
[websummit.com/tracks/growth-summit](https://websummit.com/tracks/growth-summit) 


### Health Summit
Health Summit showcases the technological innovations shaping the future of healthcare. It covers a wide range of topics, from personal health tech like wearables and remote monitoring to major breakthroughs in brain health and human enhancement.
#### Topics related to this content track include: Healthtech, Medical Devices, Wearables, Digital Health, AI in Healthcare.
[websummit.com/tracks/health-summit/](https://websummit.com/tracks/health-summit/)


### Corporate Innovation Summit
Corporate Innovation Summit is for established companies navigating rapid technological change. It addresses the challenge of fostering a genuine culture of innovation while maintaining the operational efficiency needed to succeed at scale.
#### Topics related to this content track include: Digital Transformation, Business Strategy, Operational Efficiency, Innovation Culture.
[websummit.com/tracks/corporate-innovation-summit](https://websummit.com/tracks/corporate-innovation-summit)


### New Energy Summit
New Energy Summit focuses on one of the world's most pressing issues, this track discusses sustainability and the future of energy. It explores how countries and companies can balance the critical needs of energy security, affordability, and decarbonization.
#### Topics related to this content track include: Clean Energy, Sustainability, Green Tech, Decarbonization, Climate Tech.
[websummit.com/tracks/new-energy-summit](https://websummit.com/tracks/new-energy-summit)


### People Summit
People Summit explores the evolution of the modern workplace. With flexible, hybrid setups now the norm, conversations focus on the new challenges of retaining top talent, building culture remotely, and integrating automation effectively.
#### Topics related to this content track include: Future of Work, HR Tech, Talent Retention, Remote Work, Automation.
[websummit.com/tracks/people-summit](https://websummit.com/tracks/people-summit)


### Developer Summit
Developers Summit is a track for the builders of the tech world, which focuses on the critical challenge of creating secure and scalable systems. It addresses how to protect infrastructure and user data in an environment where digital threats are constantly evolving.

### Creative Summit
